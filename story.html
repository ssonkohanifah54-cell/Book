My Story

My story begins in Uganda, a place full of colour, energy and inspiration. Growing up here has taught me to appreciate family, friendship, hard work and the beauty of everyday life.

As a student, I am always learning something new. I believe every experience, whether big or small, helps shape the person I am becoming.

Girl in Blue is my way of expressing myself: a space for my dreams, my style, my interests and my journey.
